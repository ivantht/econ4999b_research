Export trend and the real effective exchange rate

log(REER) on the lagged Hodrick-Prescott log trend of nominal exports, the controls that are significant in the paper's exchange-rate column, and country fixed effects.

Controls are lagged one month. Annual industrial production, financial development, and debt/GDP use the year of that lagged month, so January uses the previous year. GPR and WGI are omitted: GPR is not significant in that column, and the paper's central-bank-independence term is not significant either.

Standard errors are clustered by country. N = 956. Countries = 6. Months = 2006-02 to 2025-01. R-squared = 0.8540. The fixed-effect base country is Chile.

Countries in the regression: Chile, Hungary, Mexico, South Korea, Taiwan, Turkey.

No exchange-rate series: Ukraine, Egypt, Tunisia, Vietnam. Exchange rate is present, but industrial production is not, so the country is out of this complete-case sample: Argentina, India, Indonesia, Peru, Philippines, South Africa, Thailand.
