Exchange rate with the country GPR

GPR starts as the raw country article share in cleaned/gpr_country.csv. On the months that enter the regression it is standardized within each country, which is the paper's regression step. A negative coefficient is a depreciation of the real effective exchange rate.

The emerging-economy GPR coefficient is -0.0018. Table 3, column 4 is −0.010 and significant at 1 percent. The sign agrees and the significance star does not agree.

Industrial production is omitted so that Argentina, Peru, South Africa, India, Indonesia, the Philippines, and Thailand remain. Central bank independence is not in cleaned/. Financial development is fd_fdi_ovrl. Controls are lagged one month. Standard errors are clustered by country. *** p<0.01, ** p<0.05, * p<0.10.
