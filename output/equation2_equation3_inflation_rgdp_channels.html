Equation (2)/(3) rework: inflation and real-GDP channels

Sample: 10-country baseline set from equation-1 tables: Chile (CL), Hungary (HU), Indonesia (ID), India (IN), Korea (KR), Mexico (MX), Philippines (PH), Thailand (TH), Türkiye (TR), South Africa (ZA). WGI standardization: same as GPR method, within country (x - country mean) / sample SD (ddof=1).

Stage 1 channel models (for each WGI aspect): channel_it = α + β1·IQ_i,t-1 + β2·GPR_it + β3·(GPR_it×IQ_i,t-1) + ν'Controls_i,t-1 + δ_i + ε_it, with channels = inflation and real-GDP proxy growth. Stage 2: Δln(REER_it) = α + γ1·ε̂_infl,it + γ2·ε̂_rgdp,it.

Real-GDP channel uses annual industrial production growth proxy (Δln annual index) repeated within year, because monthly real GDP is not available in cleaned data. Effective overlap window is 2001-01 to 2022-01.
