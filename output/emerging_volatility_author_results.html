Emerging-market capital-flow volatility

The sample is the emerging economies in the replication file. Advanced economies are out. GPR is contemporaneous and standardized within country. The other controls, including WGI, use the previous month. WGI is the calendar year of that month, and a year missing from the file stays blank. WGI is centered before it is multiplied by GPR. Oil, VIX, global EPU, and industrial production are logged. Each cell shows the coefficient, the country-clustered standard error in parentheses, and the 95% confidence interval. *** p<0.01, ** p<0.05, * p<0.10.

The outcome is the paper's capital-flow volatility: the three-month standard deviation of portfolio inflows, in billion US dollars. It is not divided by the size of the flow. The controls are the paper's lagged controls, including industrial production and central-bank independence, plus one WGI score and its interaction with GPR. Table 2's full-sample GPR coefficient is 0.867 and is shown as a reference. That column has no WGI term and is not limited to emerging markets.
