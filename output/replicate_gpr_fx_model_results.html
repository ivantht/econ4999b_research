Replication of paper-style panel model: exchange-rate level response to geopolitical shocks

This report follows the panel-regression structure in doc/how GRP affect FX and capital flow.pdf, but uses repository shocks and screened countries.

Model used

y_it = β·GPR_it + γ'X_i,t-1 + φ'Z_t-1 + δ_i + ε_it

  • y_it: log exchange-rate level proxy, ln(local_per_usd_t), matching the paper's level-log setup for exchange rates.
  • GPR_it: repository shock proxy (event-month signed jump), including country-standardized proxy in paper-style specifications.
  • X_i,t−1: lagged domestic controls available here: log industrial production, inflation, policy rate, debt-to-GDP, financial development.
  • Z_t−1: lagged global controls per paper style: log GEPU and log VIX.
  • δ_i: country fixed effects; SE clustered by country.
Missing-data handling: no rows are dropped for missing controls. Industrial production is excluded from controls, and remaining missing controls are imputed using country-median (country controls) or global-median (global controls) values.
Alignment note: The paper uses monthly country GPR index levels and real effective exchange rate. This repository has shock episodes and bilateral local-per-USD FX; therefore GPR_it and exchange-rate measure are proxies, but the formula structure now matches Equation (2).
