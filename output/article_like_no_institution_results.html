Article-like replication model using project data (no institution terms)

Specification: ln(REER_it) = α + β·GPR_z_it + ν'Controls_i,t-1 + δ_i + ε_it
GPR source: country-specific Caldara-Iacoviello series GPRC_* from replication/raw/gpr_export.xls, standardized within country (mean 0, sample SD 1).
Controls: policy rate, ln(oil), ln(VIX), ln(GEPU), CBI, debt/GDP (all lagged where applicable).
Inflation control source: cleaned/CPI-calculated inflation.csv, with infl_l1 backed out from CPI as ((CPI_t - CPI_t-12) / CPI_t-12) * 100 and then lagged one month in the regression.
Frequency: monthly; window: 2000-01 to 2023-12.
Fixed effects: country FE; SE: clustered by country.

Institution level and interaction terms are intentionally excluded as requested.
