Inflation and output as the export channels

Each equation includes GPR, lagged WGI, and their interaction, plus country fixed effects. WGI is centered on that equation's sample, so the GPR coefficient is the effect at the average institution and the interaction is the cushion around that average.

A negative inflation interaction means a higher WGI reduces the inflation response to GPR. For the export-trend change, the interaction is how much a higher WGI changes the response of that growth rate to GPR. Standard errors in parentheses are clustered by country.

Import/GDP is not available. The log oil price is the imported-cost control. The second equation uses the year-over-year log change of the real-export Hodrick-Prescott trend. That series is already a log change.
