5. What this does and does not say

Consistent across frequencies. Volatility correlates with debt/GDP at roughly +0.22 to +0.24 in the emerging panel whether the data are monthly or quarterly. The frequency choice is not driving anything.

Mostly a cross-country fact, with a real time dimension. The first-difference correlation collapses toward zero, but the annual cross-sections stay positive, so the pattern is not merely fixed country differences.

Volatility, not volume. The correlation with the flow level is near zero to slightly negative within countries, suggesting the debt link runs through instability of flows rather than their size.

Not a regression coefficient. These are raw correlations, with no controls for GPR, inflation, the policy rate, or country fixed effects. They are not directly comparable in magnitude to the conditional debt/GDP coefficients in the model 2 specifications.
