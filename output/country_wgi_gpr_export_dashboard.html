WGI, country GPR, nominal-export log trend, and capital-flow volatility

The x-axis is fixed to the requested 2001–2023 frame. WGI is annual; GPR, the HP log trend of nominal exports, and capital-flow volatility are annual means of monthly values. Each series is indexed independently from its first available value inside the frame; a change equal to its full observed range is 100 index points. This is not a percent-growth index. Values are plotted wherever available, and gaps are not imputed. Correlations use unindexed annual values and pairwise-complete observations.
