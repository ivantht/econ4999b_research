Export trend and the real effective exchange rate

Second stage: log real effective exchange rate on the fitted lagged export trend, contemporaneous GPR, the paper's controls, and country fixed effects. GPR is included here because the paper treats it as a direct exchange-rate shock. Leaving it out of this stage would force that shock to operate only through exports. The exchange rate is cleaned/real effective exchange rate.xlsx.

First stage: the lagged Hodrick-Prescott trend of nominal exports on one lagged WGI dimension, that dimension times GPR, the same GPR term, the same controls, and country fixed effects. The trend is the ytrend sheet and is not logged again. WGI and WGI × GPR are the excluded instruments. GPR is the country article share, standardized within each country on the months in the regression. WGI is centered on this sample before the interaction, so the first-stage GPR coefficient is the export response at the average WGI score. The other controls come from cleaned/replication_cleaned and are lagged one month.

Each cell shows the coefficient, the country-clustered standard error in parentheses, and the 95% confidence interval. The standard errors use the 2SLS structural residual. *** p<0.01, ** p<0.05, * p<0.10.
