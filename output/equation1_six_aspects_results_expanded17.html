Article-aligned exchange-rate regressions with six institution aspects

Dependent variable: ln(REER_it) (log level of REER, aligned with article Eq. (2) exchange-rate specification).

Article-aligned specification used: ln(REER_it) = α + β1·Institution_i,t-1 + β2·GPR_it + β3·(GPR_it×Institution_i,t-1) + ν'Controls_i,t-1 + δ_i + ε_it
Controls (reworked set): policy rate, inflation, log oil price, log VIX, log GEPU, financial development, central bank independence, debt/GDP (all lagged 1 month). Real industrial production control is dropped in this run to retain more countries.
GPR definition: country-standardized monthly GPR (gpr_z) from article replication panel for panel countries; for added screened countries outside panel, global GPRH monthly z-score proxy from cleaned/gpr_index.csv.
Institution definition: for each aspect (va/pv/ge/rq/rl/cc), annual score from merged cleaned institution file, filled within-country (forward/backward), lagged one month, z-standardized.
Estimator: OLS with country fixed effects and clustered SE by country; 95% confidence intervals reported.
