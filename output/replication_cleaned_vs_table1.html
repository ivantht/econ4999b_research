What each row is

  • Capital flow volatility (SD). Three-month standard deviation of portfolio inflows, billion dollars.
  • Real stock prices (log). Log of the nominal share-price index, 2015=100. This is the scale of Table 1.
  • Real stock prices, CPI-deflated (log). Log of the share-price index divided by the CPI. Table 1 does not print this series.
  • Long-term bond yield (%). Long-term government bond yield.
  • Real effective exchange rate (log). Log of the broad real effective exchange rate.
  • Geopolitical risk (index). Raw country article share. The regression standardizes this within each country.
  • Policy rate (%). Central-bank policy rate. Euro-area members use the euro-area rate.
  • Real industrial production (log). Log of the industrial-production index, 2015=100.
  • Inflation (%). Year-on-year CPI inflation.
  • Oil price (log). Log of the WTI spot price, repeated for every country in a month.
  • VIX, level. Table 1 is labeled log, but the printed numbers are the VIX level. Repeated for every country.
  • VIX, log. Natural log of the VIX. Table 1 does not print these moments.
  • Global economic policy uncertainty (index). GEPU current index, the level, repeated for every country in a month.
  • Financial development (index). IMF financial-development index, 0 to 1. Annual, repeated within the year.
  • Central bank independence (index). Romelli central-bank independence index. Annual, repeated within the year.
  • Public debt to GDP (%). General-government gross debt as a percent of GDP. Annual, repeated within the year.
