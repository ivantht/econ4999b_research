What each row is

  • Capital flow volatility (SD). Cleaned file is the quarterly dollar flow, not a 3-month standard deviation of portfolio inflows.
  • Real stock prices (log). No stock-price series in cleaned.
  • Long-term bond yield (%). No bond-yield series in cleaned.
  • Real effective exchange rate (log). Log of the cleaned real effective exchange rate.
  • Geopolitical risk (index). Raw country article share in gpr_country.csv, restricted here to 2000-2023. The author's regression then standardizes this within each country.
  • Geopolitical risk, global index. Series GPR in gpr_index.csv. One global series scaled near 100, not the country article share.
  • Policy rate (%). Monthly policy rate.
  • Real industrial production (log). Cleaned index is annual. The paper's row is the log of a monthly index.
  • Inflation (%). Monthly year-on-year inflation.
  • Oil price (log). Log of the monthly WTI spot price. One observation per month.
  • VIX, level. Table 1 is labeled log, but its mean of 19.59 and maximum of 62.67 are the level. One observation per month.
  • VIX, log. Natural log of the cleaned VIX. These moments are not the numbers printed in Table 1.
  • Global economic policy uncertainty (index). GEPU current index, the level. One observation per month.
  • Financial development (index). Column fd_fdi_ovrl. The paper uses the IMF index on a 0-to-1 scale.
  • Central bank independence (index). Romelli's index is not in cleaned.
  • Public debt to GDP (%). Annual general-government gross debt as a percent of GDP.
