Annual GPR–portfolio-inflow correlation and average WGI, emerging economies

Each point is one of the 14 emerging economies. The horizontal axis is that country's average WGI score. The vertical axis is the country's annual correlation between standardized GPR and portfolio inflow. The correlation in the chart title is the Pearson correlation across those 14 economies. The gray line is the fitted line.

The WGI average uses 2000 and 2002 through 2023. WGI is blank in 2001. Source: cleaned/replication_cleaned/country_month.csv.
