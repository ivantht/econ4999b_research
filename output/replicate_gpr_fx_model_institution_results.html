New section: S2 extended with institution interaction

Base model follows S2, extended with a standardized institution term and interaction.

y_it = β1·GPR_it + β2·Institution_it + β3·(GPR_it×Institution_it) + γ'Controls + δ_i + ε_it

  • Institution level: yearly WGI composite (mean across available six dimensions), then carried forward by country for missing years.
  • Institution standardization: pooled z-score over the panel.
  • Interaction tested: GPR × Institution.
  • Controls: inflation, policy rate, debt/GDP, financial development, log GEPU, log VIX (industrial production excluded).
Missing-data handling: no rows dropped for control missingness; controls imputed with country/global medians. Institution missing years are carry-forwarded first, then any residual missing uses median imputation.

Interaction significance test

No significant interaction at conventional levels (p=0.1082). This suggests limited evidence that institution level modifies the GPR effect in this specification.
