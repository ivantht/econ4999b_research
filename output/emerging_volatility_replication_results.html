Emerging-market capital-flow volatility

The sample is the emerging economies in the replication file. The outcome is the three-month standard deviation of gross portfolio inflows, column port_inf in the September 2026 OECD Monthly Capital Flow Dataset, in billion US dollars. On the 6,505 months from 2000 through 2023, that series matches capital_flow_volatility in the replication file. Months before 2000 or after the controls end do not enter. GPR is contemporaneous and standardized within country on the months in that column. The other controls use the previous month. Oil, VIX, global EPU, and industrial production are logged.

Country fixed effects are estimated via within-country demeaning. Consistent with run_replication_cleaned_volatility_wgi.py, both conventional within standard errors (treating months as independent, dof = N - k - G) and country-clustered standard errors (clustering by country, dof = G - 1) are reported. *** p<0.01, ** p<0.05, * p<0.10.

The first column is Equation (2), with no WGI term. Table 3's emerging GPR coefficient is 3.144 (standard error 1.840), with 1,569 observations and an R-squared of 0.011. This OECD extract's volatility reaches 412, while Table 1's maximum is 528. The other columns add one centered WGI score and its interaction with GPR. WGI is the calendar year of the lagged month, and a missing year drops that month from that column only.
