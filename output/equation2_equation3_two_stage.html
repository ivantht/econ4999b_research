The only located series is C:\0 UST CourseWork\ECON4999B\MacroEconResearch_GPR-FX-Institution\cleaned\exports_three_category.csv. It covers Chile, Indonesia, Malaysia, Peru, Philippines, South Africa, and Thailand, from 2018. Malaysia and Peru are outside the 10-country sample and are not added. Of the 10 equation-1 countries, only Chile (CL), Indonesia (ID), Philippines (PH), Thailand (TH), South Africa (ZA) have an export series. Dropped for missing exports: Hungary (HU), India (IN), Korea (KR), Mexico (MX), Türkiye (TR). Even for those five countries, the estimable window is 2018-01 to 2022-01, not 2000-2023. It ends in January 2022 because lagged financial development in the article replication panel is missing from February 2022. Later financial-development values in the cleaned file were not substituted, so the controls stay the same as equation 1. Indonesia, the Philippines, South Africa, and Thailand also have no export observation in January 2018. The estimates below are a partial overlap test, not the requested full-sample result.

What is estimated

Target sample: the equation-1 complete-case countries (Chile (CL), Hungary (HU), Indonesia (ID), India (IN), Korea (KR), Mexico (MX), Philippines (PH), Thailand (TH), Türkiye (TR), South Africa (ZA)). WGI is standardized within country by the article GPR method: (x - country mean) / sample SD, with sample standard deviation (ddof=1), on the lagged monthly institution value over 2000-2023. There is no separate IQ series, so IQ is that standardized WGI aspect. The interaction uses the same standardized aspect.

Equation 2: ln(Export_it) = α + β1·IQ_i,t-1 + β2·GPR_it + β3·(GPR_it×IQ_i,t-1) + ν'Controls_i,t-1 + δ_i + ε_it
Equation 3: Δln(REER_it) = α + β1·ε̂_it, using the equation-2 residual. Equation 3 has no extra controls and no country fixed effects, matching the written equation. Both stages use country-clustered standard errors and 95% confidence intervals. Stars: * 10%, ** 5%, *** 1%.

Export construction

Export is the sum, in USD millions, of merchandise categories that are present in every observed month for that country. The regression uses ln(Export). A raw export level is not used, because country fixed effects would otherwise be dominated by scale. The basket is not the same in every country.

None of these reaches 5% in this run.
