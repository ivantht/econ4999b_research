What each row is

  • Capital flow volatility (SD). 3-month standard deviation of portfolio inflows, in billion dollars. This is the volatility outcome.
  • Stock prices (log). Log of the nominal equity index. The CPI-deflated series used in a stock regression has mean about -0.25, so it is not the Table 1 row.
  • Long-term bond yield (%). Long-term government bond yield.
  • Real effective exchange rate (log). Log of the BIS broad real effective exchange rate.
  • Geopolitical risk (index). Raw country article share, the series printed in Table 1. The regression standardizes this within each country, which gives mean 0 and standard deviation 1.
  • Policy rate (%). BIS policy rate.
  • Real industrial production (log). Log of the monthly industrial-production index.
  • Inflation (%). Year-on-year CPI inflation.
  • Oil price (log). Log of the oil price deflated by US CPI, with January 2000 as the base. The regression uses the nominal log, whose mean is about 4.05.
  • VIX, level. Table 1 is labeled log, but the printed numbers are the VIX level.
  • Global economic policy uncertainty (index). GEPU current index, the level, repeated for every country in a month.
  • Financial development (index). IMF financial-development index, 0 to 1.
  • Central bank independence (index). Romelli central-bank independence index.
  • Public debt to GDP (%). General-government gross debt as a percent of GDP.
