Institutions and the GPR–volatility correlation

Emerging economies · 13 countries, excluding China and Hungary · each country’s monthly r between gpr_z and capital_flow_volatility, paired with its average WGI score

None of these six correlations is statistically distinguishable from zero. With n = 13 countries, the 5% two-sided critical value is |r| = 0.509. The strongest relationship here is |r| = 0.296, which is still far short of it. The honest reading is that this sample carries essentially no information about whether institutional quality moderates the GPR–volatility link.

Why this is uninformative, not a finding

The outcome is already noisy. The country-level correlations being correlated here run from −0.271 to +0.130 and were estimated from between 106 and 288 monthly observations. Each is measured with substantial error, so the regressand in this exercise is mostly estimation noise. Attenuation bias pushes all six coefficients toward zero.

The earlier version of this table was driven by two points. With China and Hungary included, voice and accountability read −0.307 and five dimensions leaned positive. Remove those two countries and voice and accountability becomes +0.046 while government effectiveness, regulatory quality and control of corruption all strengthen slightly. That reshuffling is the signature of a coefficient resting on two observations, not on a cross-country relationship.

What the remaining pattern means. The three governance dimensions that now lead — government effectiveness, control of corruption, regulatory quality — sit between +0.26 and +0.30, still well short of the 0.509 threshold, and the two political dimensions collapse to roughly zero. All six would need to move together, in the same direction and past the threshold, before this could be called a moderating effect.

What would actually test the claim. Interact GPR with WGI inside a panel regression with country and time fixed effects, so the test uses the 2,849 country-months rather than 13 country averages. That is the specification the earlier tables in this file move toward.
