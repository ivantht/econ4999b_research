Redone regression using article-defined variables + institution score from cleaned dataset

Base macro/financial variables follow the article-style panel pipeline in econ4999b_literature_review/replication/raw (via existing panel file), with one extension: a continuous institution score from cleaned/wgi.csv.

Institution variable construction: annual country-year mean of available WGI dimension scores in cleaned/wgi.csv; merged to monthly panel by year, filled within country-year sequence using forward/backward carry, then lagged one month and standardized (z-score) for regression.
Standardization stats: mean=-0.030222, std=0.542824.
