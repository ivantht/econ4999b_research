Quarterly log REER change regression without controls

For each WGI measure, estimate a separate country-fixed-effects regression: Δln(REER)i,t or t+1 = αi + β₁ GPRi,t + β₂ (GPRi,t × average WGIi) + εi,t. Use the outcome-timing selector above to switch between the two outcome quarters.

The outcome is the stored quarterly d_log_reer, the sum of three monthly log changes. GPR is the stored quarterly gpr_z. Average WGI is the same country-specific 2000–2023 annual average used in the scatterplots, in original units; its interaction with GPR is not centered or standardized. Only complete pairs with an available country average enter. The WGI main effect is omitted because it is constant within country and absorbed by the fixed effects. No additional controls are included.

The WGI average uses the full period, including years after some observations, so the results are descriptive rather than a real-time or causal analysis. Each coefficient is followed by its country-clustered standard error in parentheses. The cluster correction is G/(G−1) × (N−1)/(N−k), where k includes the country fixed effects and two slopes. Within R² measures fit after removing country means. The GPR coefficient is the association at average WGI = 0.

Definitions and sample

Source: cleaned/data_for_quarter_model.csv. Quarterly gpr_z is the mean of three monthly within-country standardized GPR values; d_log_reer is the sum of three monthly log REER changes. A positive correlation means higher GPR aligns with a larger rise in log REER, not necessarily a causal effect.

Same-quarter pairs use 2000Q2–2023Q4; next-quarter pairs use GPR in 2000Q1–2023Q3 and REER changes in 2000Q2–2023Q4. The first quarter's REER change is missing and 2023Q4 has no following quarter. Only non-missing, consecutive-quarter pairs are included.

Each country's WGI average gives equal weight to available annual scores in 2000–2023, in original units. The 2001 scores are missing. Both the scatterplots and the regression use this full-period average, unlike the lagged WGI in Model 1.
