Quarterly GPR–REER correlation and average WGI, emerging economies

Each point is one emerging economy. The horizontal axis is that country's average WGI score. The vertical axis is the country's quarterly correlation between standardized GPR and the change in log REER. The correlation in the chart title is the Pearson correlation across the 15 emerging economies. The gray line is the fitted line.

The WGI average uses 2000 and 2002 through 2023. WGI is blank in 2001. Source: cleaned/replication_cleaned/country_month.csv.
