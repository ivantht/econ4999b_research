Export trend and the real effective exchange rate

Second stage: log(REER) on the fitted Hodrick-Prescott log trend of nominal exports and country fixed effects.

First stage: the export trend on the excluded instruments and country fixed effects. The instruments do not enter the second stage. GPR is contemporaneous. Every other instrument is lagged one month. Annual series use the year of that lagged month, so January uses the previous year.

The monthly export trend uses lambda 129600. That scaling is not from the lecture. Standard errors are clustered by country and use the 2SLS structural residual. Each cell shows the coefficient, the standard error in parentheses, and the 95% confidence interval.

Countries with no real effective exchange rate are omitted: Ukraine, Egypt, Tunisia, Vietnam.
