REER regressions with six institution aspects (two tables)

Exchange-rate variable check: both tables use only real effective exchange rate (REER) as Y (ln(REER_it)). No nominal bilateral FX series are used in these two tables.

Common model: ln(REER_it) = α + β1·Institution_i,t-1 + β2·GPR_it + β3·(GPR_it×Institution_i,t-1) + ν'Controls_i,t-1 + δ_i + ε_it. Controls: policy rate, inflation, log oil price, log VIX, log GEPU, financial development, central bank independence, debt/GDP (lagged 1 month).
