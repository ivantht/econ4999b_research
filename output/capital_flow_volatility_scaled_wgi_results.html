Volatility as a share of the inflow

The outcome is the 3-month standard deviation of portfolio inflows divided by the absolute value of the 3-month mean inflow. A coefficient is a change in that ratio. A month is dropped when the absolute mean inflow is below 0.1 billion dollars.

GPR is the country index standardized within each country, in the same month. Each regression uses one WGI dimension, taken from the year of the previous month and centered on this sample. The GPR coefficient is the effect at that average. The interaction is the extra GPR effect when WGI is one point higher.

Real industrial production is omitted. The other controls are lagged one month and include central bank independence. Standard errors are clustered by country. *** p<0.01, ** p<0.05, * p<0.10.
