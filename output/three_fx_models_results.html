Three exchange-rate models

Ukraine and Thailand are dropped. GPR is contemporaneous and standardized within country. The other controls, including WGI, use the previous month. WGI is centered before it is multiplied by GPR. Each cell shows the coefficient, the country-clustered standard error in parentheses, and the 95% confidence interval. Second-stage standard errors use the structural residual. *** p<0.01, ** p<0.05, * p<0.10.

Industrial-production growth is the year-over-year percent change in the replication file, lagged one month. Nominal GDP and total nominal imports are not in the files, so nominal imports divided by nominal GDP are not in Model 3. The real-export growth rate is the year-over-year percent change after its cycle is removed with lambda 129,600. That monthly scaling is not from the lecture.
