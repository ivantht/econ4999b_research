Three exchange-rate models

Ukraine and Thailand are dropped. GPR is contemporaneous and standardized within country. The other controls, including WGI, use the previous month. WGI is centered before it is multiplied by GPR. Each cell shows the coefficient, the country-clustered standard error in parentheses, and the 95% confidence interval. Standard errors after the first stage use the structural residual. *** p<0.01, ** p<0.05, * p<0.10.

Industrial-production growth is the year-over-year percent change in the replication file, lagged one month. There is no monthly GDP series. The import share is the annual trend ratio of imports to nominal GDP, repeated within the year, and January uses the previous year. The nominal-export outcome and the real-export outcome are Hodrick-Prescott log trends, so they are not logged again.
