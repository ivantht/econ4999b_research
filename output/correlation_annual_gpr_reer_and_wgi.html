Annual GPR–REER correlation and average WGI

Each point is one country. The horizontal axis is that country's average WGI score. The vertical axis is the country's annual correlation between standardized GPR and the change in log REER. The correlation in the chart title is the Pearson correlation across the 29 countries. The gray line is the fitted line. Blue points are advanced economies and rust points are emerging economies.

The WGI average uses 2000 and 2002 through 2023. WGI is blank in 2001. Source: cleaned/replication_cleaned/country_month.csv.
