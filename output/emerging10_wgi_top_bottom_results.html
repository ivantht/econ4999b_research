Ten emerging economies: GPR and WGI, top three and bottom three

The ranking uses the ten emerging economies that have at least two complete months. Indonesia, Philippines, South Africa, Thailand, Ukraine are removed before ranking because each lacks a required control in every month. The equation is country-standardized GPR, the lagged WGI score in its original units, their interaction, the lagged controls, and country fixed effects. WGI is not centered. The GPR coefficient is the effect when the WGI score is zero, and the interaction is the change in that effect for a one-point higher WGI score. GPR is standardized within each country on the months that enter that regression. Each observation is a country-month from January 2000 through December 2023, kept only when the outcome and every regressor are observed. The top three and the bottom three are estimated separately. An exact tie at the third-place cutoff keeps every country on that cutoff.
