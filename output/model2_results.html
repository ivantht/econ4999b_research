Reading the table

  • The outcome is in billions of dollars, so a coefficient is a change in that unit.
  • GPR is standardized within country, so its coefficient is the effect of a one-standard-deviation rise in geopolitical risk.
  • The interaction is the change in that GPR slope per one point of the lagged WGI score. Its coefficient is small relative to the spread of the WGI scores in most columns.
  • Only the lagged log oil-price change is significant at 10 percent in all six columns. GPR and the interaction are significant in none, and the lagged WGI score in one (government effectiveness, p = 0.057).
