Capital flow, emerging economies

Each panel is one emerging economy that appears in capital_flow.csv. Turkey is labeled Turkiye. Argentina, Egypt, Peru, South Korea, Tunisia, and Vietnam are in that file and outside the emerging sample, so they are omitted. Brazil, China, Colombia, Malaysia, and Poland are emerging and have no capital-flow row. The number after each country is the count of observed quarters. Each panel has its own vertical scale. The horizontal line is zero. Zooming one panel moves the time window on the others.
