Capital-flow volatility on GPR and WGI

The outcome is the standard deviation of portfolio inflows over three months, the author's volatility measure. It is not the quarterly dollar flow.

GPR is the country index standardized within each country, in the same month. Each regression uses one WGI dimension from cleaned/wgi.csv, taken from the year of the previous month and centered on this sample. The GPR coefficient is the effect at that average. The interaction is the extra GPR effect when WGI is one point higher.

Real industrial production is omitted. The other controls are lagged one month and include central bank independence. Standard errors are clustered by country. *** p<0.01, ** p<0.05, * p<0.10.
