Capital flow on GPR and WGI

The outcome is the quarterly dollar flow in cleaned/capital_flow.csv. Negative values stay negative. This is not a volatility and it is not a log.

GPR is the average of the raw country article share over the same quarter, then standardized within each country on the quarters that enter the regression. Each regression uses one WGI dimension, lagged to the year of the previous quarter and centered on this sample. The GPR coefficient is the effect at that average. The interaction is the extra GPR effect when WGI is one point higher. Regulatory quality, government effectiveness, and rule of law are estimated separately.

The other controls are lagged one quarter. Real industrial production is omitted so that countries without the annual index remain in the sample. Central bank independence is not in cleaned/. Standard errors are clustered by country. *** p<0.01, ** p<0.05, * p<0.10.
