Annual GPR–capital-flow correlation and average WGI, emerging economies

Each point is one emerging economy with a capital-flow series. The horizontal axis is that country's average WGI score. The vertical axis is the country's annual correlation between standardized GPR and capital flow. The correlation in the chart title is the Pearson correlation across those nine economies. The gray line is the fitted line.

The WGI average uses 2000 and 2002 through 2023. WGI is blank in 2001. Brazil, China, Colombia, Malaysia, and Poland have no capital-flow row. Source: cleaned/replication_cleaned/country_month.csv and cleaned/capital_flow.csv.
