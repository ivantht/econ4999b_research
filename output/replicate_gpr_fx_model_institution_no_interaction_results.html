S2 extended with institution (no interaction): two separate regressions

y_it = β1·GPR_it + β2·Institution_it + γ'Controls + δ_i + ε_it

  • Dependent variable: monthly BIS real effective exchange rate (real, broad basket), transformed as ln(REER_t).
  • Regression A institution variable: Rule of Law (WGI rl)
  • Regression B institution variable: Government Effectiveness (WGI ge)
  • Each institution series is carry-forwarded by country-year for missing years, then standardized (z-score) within its own regression panel.
  • Controls: inflation, policy rate, debt/GDP, financial development, log GEPU, log VIX (industrial production excluded).
Requested means: separate institution means are calculated and shown below for rule of law and government effectiveness.
