Three exchange-rate models, all six WGI dimensions

Ukraine and Thailand are dropped. Each column is one of the six Worldwide Governance Indicators. GPR is contemporaneous and standardized within country. The other controls, including WGI, use the previous month. WGI is centered before it is multiplied by GPR. Each cell shows the coefficient, the country-clustered standard error in parentheses, and the 95% confidence interval. Standard errors after the first stage use the structural residual. *** p<0.01, ** p<0.05, * p<0.10.

Industrial-production growth is the year-over-year percent change in the replication file, lagged one month. There is no monthly GDP series. The import share is the annual trend ratio of imports to nominal GDP, repeated within the year, and January uses the previous year. The nominal-export outcome is the Hodrick-Prescott log trend of the monthly nominal-export workbook. The real-export outcome is the Hodrick-Prescott log trend. Neither series is logged again. Oil, VIX, and global EPU enter as logs, as in Equation (2).
