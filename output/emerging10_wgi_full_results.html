Ten emerging economies: GPR and WGI, full sample

One regression uses all ten emerging economies that have at least two complete months: Brazil, Chile, China, Colombia, Hungary, India, Malaysia, Mexico, Poland, Türkiye. Indonesia, Philippines, South Africa, Thailand, Ukraine are left out before estimation because each lacks a required control in every month. The equation is country-standardized GPR, the lagged WGI score in its original units, their interaction, the lagged controls, and country fixed effects. WGI is not centered. The GPR coefficient is the effect when the WGI score is zero, and the interaction is the change in that effect for a one-point higher WGI score. GPR is standardized within each country on the months that enter that regression. Each observation is a country-month from January 2000 through December 2023, kept only when the outcome and every regressor are observed. There is no top or bottom split. Each WGI aspect is estimated separately.
