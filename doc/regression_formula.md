$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta\text{Exchange rate}
&= \alpha + \beta_1\text{institution} + \beta_2\text{GPR} \\
&\quad + \beta_3(\text{GPR}\times\text{institution})
+ \vec{\nu}\,\text{Control}
\end{aligned}
\tag{1}
$$

If (1) is insignificant:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Export}
&= \alpha + \beta_1\text{IQ} + \beta_2\text{GPR} \\
&\quad + \beta_3(\text{GPR}\times\text{institution})
+ \vec{\nu}\,\text{Control} + \hat{\varepsilon}
\end{aligned}
\tag{2}
$$

Use the same control variables in (1) and (2).

$$
\Delta\text{Exchange rate}
= \alpha + \beta_1\hat{\varepsilon}
\tag{3}
$$

If (2) and (3) are insignificant:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\pi
&= \alpha + \beta_1\text{IQ} + \beta_2\text{GPR} \\
&\quad + \beta_3(\text{GPR}\times\text{institution})
+ \vec{\nu}\,\text{Control} + \hat{\varepsilon}_1
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{q/real GDP}
&= \alpha + \beta_1\text{IQ} + \beta_2\text{GPR} \\
&\quad + \beta_3(\text{GPR}\times\text{institution})
+ \vec{\nu}\,\text{Control} + \hat{\varepsilon}_2
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\text{Export}
= \alpha + \beta_1\hat{\varepsilon}_1
+ \beta_2\hat{\varepsilon}_2 + \hat{\varepsilon}_3
$$

$$
\Delta\text{Exchange rate}
= \alpha + \beta_1\hat{\varepsilon}_3
$$
